About Me

Hi there! I bring over four years of hands-on experience in pet hospitality, caring for dogs of all ages, sizes, breeds, and needs. I have experience with energetic breeds, senior pups requiring additional support, medication management, mobility challenges, and veterinary visits.

Throughout my life, I have owned and loved five dogs, giving me a deep understanding of the trust involved when someone welcomes you into their home to care for their family member.

Beyond pet care, I have also been trusted to care for luxury residences throughout Coto de Caza, Dove Canyon, Ladera Ranch, Irvine, Newport, and surrounding communities. My responsibilities have included managing weekly household needs, coordinating with maids, landscapers, pool services, and vendors while maintaining homes with professionalism, discretion, and care.

Whether it is keeping up with energetic huskies, providing compassionate care for senior dogs, or ensuring your home remains secure while you are away, you can count on me to handle everything with reliability and professionalism.

Rest assured — I have you and your pets covered.
